The screenshots in this folder show the Wildlife Scanner web app running on its published data
for Monterey Bay and San Diego (November 2026).

- **Maps:** © OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors.
- **Map values, lists and calendars:** derived from GBIF and iNaturalist records under CC BY,

- **Species photos:** the app shows openly licensed iNaturalist photos with their credits. They
  are replaced by the app's placeholder images in these screenshots, so no third-party photos
  are included in the repository.
